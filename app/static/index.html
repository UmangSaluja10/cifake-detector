A CNN-based classifier that flags AI-generated images by spotting the small visual artifacts diffusion models leave behind.

Scope note: trained only on CIFAR-10 object categories (animals, vehicles, etc.) at 32×32 resolution using Stable Diffusion 1.4 fakes. Images outside those categories, at high resolution, or from newer generators are out-of-distribution and may be misclassified.
Method based on Bird & Lotfi, "CIFAKE" — IEEE Access, 2024
